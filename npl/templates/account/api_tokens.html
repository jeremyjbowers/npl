What the key reaches

An agent connects to {{ mcp_url }}. There is no OAuth step. The same key works on the JSON paths below, which is the way to curl one lookup. A check that the key is live:

curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer npl_…" {{ mcp_url }}/health

WAR on a player is his full MLB season, whichever NPL club he played for. forty_man is the 40-man count. reserve is the 85-man count.

Lookups

A read key can call these. A read-write key can call them too.

Tool JSON What you get
get_season_context GET /api/mcp/v1/season/ Season, phase, cap, and your club
list_teams GET /api/mcp/v1/teams/ Clubs, division, payroll, cap space, cash, and IFA
get_team_roster GET /api/mcp/v1/roster/ Roster sections. /api/mcp/v1/teams/MCP/roster/ is another club
get_roster_compliance GET /api/mcp/v1/compliance/ 40-man and 85-man counts against the limits
search_players GET /api/mcp/v1/players/search/ Name, position, club, and level. sort=war ranks by full-season MLB WAR
list_free_agents GET /api/mcp/v1/free-agents/ Unowned players, best full-season MLB WAR first
get_player GET /api/mcp/v1/players/<mlb_id>/ Contract, WAR, club-split sim lines, and the NPL clubs he has been on
list_draft_picks GET /api/mcp/v1/draft-picks/ Picks by year, season, type, or club. held=true is unused picks
list_pick_inventory GET /api/mcp/v1/pick-inventory/ Unused draft picks grouped by the club that holds them
list_trades GET /api/mcp/v1/trades/ Players, draft picks, and cash each club received
list_transactions GET /api/mcp/v1/transactions/ The posted ledger, with club names, pick text, and cash
get_my_wishlist GET /api/mcp/v1/wishlist/ Your ranks, tiers, notes, and tags
list_auctions GET /api/mcp/v1/auctions/ Open auctions. Other clubs' bids stay hidden
list_team_ledger GET /api/mcp/v1/ledger/ Contract payroll, plus cash and IFA adjustments

Filings

These need a read-write key. option_minors sends a player down. recall_option calls him up. non_tender nontenders him. Pass player as the mlb_id. Omit team when you own one club, and pass an abbreviation when you own more than one. A trade passes counterparty as the other club's abbreviation. A claim on an open waiver is claim_waiver.

Tool JSON What it does
list_transaction_kinds GET /api/v1/transactions/kinds/?fileable=1 Codes a club can file
list_transaction_proposals GET /api/v1/transactions/proposals/ Proposals your club filed or still has to answer. This is separate from the posted ledger
get_transaction_proposal GET /api/v1/transactions/proposals/<id>/ One proposal, the pieces, and who has agreed
create_transaction_proposal POST /api/v1/transactions/proposals/ File a nontender, trade, send-down, call-up, release, injured-list move, signing, or waiver request
agree_transaction POST /api/v1/transactions/proposals/<id>/agree/ Agree to a proposal waiting on your club
decline_transaction POST /api/v1/transactions/proposals/<id>/decline/ Decline a proposal waiting on your club
withdraw_transaction POST /api/v1/transactions/proposals/<id>/withdraw/ Take back a proposal your club filed
list_open_waivers GET /api/v1/waivers/ Open waivers
claim_waiver POST /api/v1/waivers/<id>/claim/ Claim a player on an open waiver
